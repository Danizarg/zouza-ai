# Zouza — problem → comfort campaign (writers' room output, 2026-10-08)

Scripts were written from four angles, ranked by three judges and corrected by an adversarial compliance pass. Pricing facts: owners €20/month flat, buyers/tenants free, no transaction fees, Zouza never handles money.

### It Rings Out  `tenant-buyer-3`

**Audience:** Tenants and buyers in Spain, 28-50, actively enquiring on listings and getting no answer; people who have one practical question (lift, parking, noise) before they will bother with a viewing.

**Problem:** Nobody answers: the phone rings out, the second number goes to voicemail, 'is it still available' sits on two blue ticks for days, and your one practical question about the lift and the parking never gets asked.

**Comfort:** Every listing on Zouza has Suzi on it. Ask about the beach, parking, light, noise or stairs and she answers from that listing's own details. Then you contact the owner directly and request a viewing.

### The Second Price  `traveller-1`

**Problem:** The price that grows on the last screen: €90 a night in the results becomes a different number once the platform's own lines land (service fee, booking fee, payment fee), with a pay button waiting under the new total.

**Comfort:** Zouza adds no fee of its own and has no checkout at all. The price on the listing is the owner's price, the traveller pays the owner and never Zouza, and 100% of it stays between them. Travellers never pay Zouza anything.
